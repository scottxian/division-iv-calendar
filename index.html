OUR DIVISION

Many lodges.
One Division.

Division IV brings together Masonic lodges organized into districts, promoting excellence in ritual, lodge administration, Masonic education, and leadership. Each district is served by an Inspector who provides guidance, instruction, and support to its constituent lodges, helping them uphold Masonic standards and traditions.

District Inspectors report to the Assistant Grand Lecturer, who works under the direction of the Grand Lecturer and Grand Master of the Grand Lodge of California. Together, this leadership structure strengthens our lodges through education, mentorship, ritual proficiency, and effective administration while fostering fellowship and cooperation throughout the Division.
