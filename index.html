OUR DIVISION

Tradition in common.
Community in action.

Division IV is a division of the Grand Lodge of California—part of the Masons of California. We bring neighboring lodges and their members together for Masonic education, fellowship, events, and service.

This website makes it easier to learn what's happening, support neighboring lodges, and find useful information in one place.

For statewide Masonic programs, membership, and official resources, visit Masons of California ↗.
