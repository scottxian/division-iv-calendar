DIVISION IV EMBROIDERY ARTWORK — PRODUCTION NOTES

Files:
- division_iv_embroidery_patch.svg and .png: 90 mm / approx. 3.54 inch patch or garment back emblem, with N/E/S/W directions.
- division_iv_embroidery_polo.svg and .png: simplified chest or hat emblem; omit compass direction letters at smaller sizes.

This is flat-color vector *artwork for digitization*. SVG/PNG are NOT embroidery-machine stitch files.
An embroidery vendor must convert artwork to machine stitches (DST, PES, EXP, etc.) for their particular machine, garment, backing, and size.

Three suggested thread colors (approximate design references; match actual commercial thread swatches):
- Deep navy background: #082342
- Warm gold square, compasses, rim, and IV: #E3B54A
- Muted antique gold compass rose: #9B845C

Embroidery guidelines:
- Size full patch at least 3.25–3.5 inches diameter to preserve cardinal lettering.
- For approximately 1.75–2.25 inch polo logo, use the simpler polo version without compass letters.
- No gradients, bevels, shadows, hairline ticks, or tiny ornamentation.
- The gold-on-navy layers have intentionally thick strokes. Adjust stitch density, pull compensation, underlay, satin widths and minimum letter size during digitizing.
- On a navy garment, the background navy may be omitted at the vendor's discretion; for a patch, stitch the navy fill or use navy twill.
- Approve a physical sew-out before a production run.

Primary web emblem and browser favicon remain separate artwork, with finer digital detail.
